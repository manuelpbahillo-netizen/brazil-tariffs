Did Lula lose more votes in the Brazilian states most exposed to the new US tariff?

A look at Brazil's 27 states: how much of what each one sells to the United States pays a new tariff, and how Lula's share of the vote changed between the 2022 and 2026 elections.

What is this about?
  • The tariff. In July 2026, the United States began charging an extra 25% tax (a "tariff") on many goods imported from Brazil.
  • Who pays. Not everything is taxed. Coffee, beef, orange juice, oil and civil aircraft are exempt, and steel and aluminum pay a separate, older tariff. So the new tariff hits some Brazilian states much harder than others.
  • The question. Brazil held the first round of its presidential election on October 4, 2026. Did President Lula lose more votes, compared with 2022, in the states most exposed to the new tariff?

What the chart shows
  • There is no clear link between how exposed a state is to the tariff and how its vote changed.

Hover over a circle to see the state's data. Labels are shown only for the biggest exporters. States that export very little to the US can show extreme percentages, which is why circle size matters.

How we measured exposure: we took each state's 2025 exports to the US and removed the products the US government exempted from the new tariff (such as coffee, beef, orange juice, oil, civil aircraft and pig iron) and the products that already pay a separate US tariff on steel, aluminum, copper, cars and pine lumber. What remains is the share that pays the new 25% tariff. The classification is approximate and was done by product code. The change in vote compares Lula's share of valid votes in the first round of 2022 and 2026. With 27 states and a single election, this chart cannot separate the tariff's effect from other factors, such as regional differences.
Sources: Comex Stat (Brazil's Ministry of Development), 2025 exports; US Trade Representative, Notice of Action of July 15, 2026; TSE (Brazil's electoral court), official first-round results for 2022 and 2026. Analysis: Manuel Bahillo.
